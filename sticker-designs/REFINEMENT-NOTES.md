# MaxRez refinements

The three current sticker PNG files are the authoritative artwork. Earlier packaging mockups are concept images and do not include the latest changes.

- Square: 7 × 7 cm, 827 × 827 pixels at 300 DPI.
- Rectangle: 10 × 5 cm, 1181 × 591 pixels at 300 DPI.
- Portrait: 8 × 12 cm, 945 × 1417 pixels at 300 DPI.
- QR destination: https://maxrez.cc (decoded and verified for each file).
- Every size includes the phone, email, Telegram handle and office address.
- Preview board now shows all three without overlap; displayed sizes are illustrative.

These PNGs are trim-size artwork, without bleed or a printer-specific colour profile. Print a physical proof at 100% size before a full run; confirm small text, QR scanning and the warm background on the chosen stock.
